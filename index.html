FRESH ANSWERS.

Something else on your mind?

Just drop us a message
Where do you cover?

C&C Valeting covers Carlisle and the surrounding areas. Include your postcode in your Instagram message so Kade can confirm whether your location is covered.

How much does a clean cost?

Ask for a quote for your specific car and the clean you have in mind. Share the make and model, its condition and any areas that need extra attention. Prices and inclusions are confirmed directly before booking.

How do I book?

Send a direct message to @c.cvaleting on Instagram. You can use the enquiry builder below to prepare your message. Your appointment is only booked once C&C Valeting confirms it with you.

Do I need to provide water or power?

Please check access requirements with Kade before your appointment. Mention where your car will be parked and whether water or power is available, so the visit can be planned properly.

Can I see recent work?

Visit the C&C Valeting Instagram profile for the company’s own posts. The photographs on this demo website are generated concept images, not customer work.
