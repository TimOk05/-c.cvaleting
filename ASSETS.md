# Asset provenance

All images were created for this independent website concept with the built-in image generation tool. They depict illustrative vehicles and cleaning, not actual C&C Valeting jobs or premises.

- `assets/hero.webp`, `assets/hero-card.webp`, `assets/social.jpg`: silver performance coupe in a dark graphite studio, front three-quarter view, reflective floor, subtle lime light, negative space at left, no lettering.
- `assets/exterior.webp`: black-gloved hand cleaning a silver car's alloy wheel with a detailing brush, foam and water droplets, overcast outdoor light.
- `assets/interior.webp`: close-up of a clean black leather sports car interior, tactile stitching, centre console and a folded microfibre cloth, natural light.

Full generation prompts are included in `assets/image-prompts.txt`. Web assets were resized and compressed for delivery. Original generated PNG files remain in the creator's local image-generation folder.

Barlow Condensed and DM Sans are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Locally hosted font subsets originate from Google Fonts. The applicable licenses are included alongside the font files.

The wordmark, favicon and interface icons are original code-native graphics for the proposal, not an assertion of the business's approved identity.
